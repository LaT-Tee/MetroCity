隐私政策

地铁织城 · 更新于 2026 年 11 月

我们不收集什么

游戏不需要注册账号，不要求你提供姓名、邮箱或任何个人信息，也不会把你的存档上传到我们的服务器。

保存在你浏览器里的东西

  • 游戏存档，以及语言、音效、音乐开关等设置，保存在浏览器的本地存储（localStorage）里，只在你的设备上，清除浏览器数据即可删除。
  • 规划助手的解锁时间，同样只保存在本地。
  • 车辆靠左还是靠右行驶，是根据你设备上的时区和语言在本地判断的，这些信息不会被发送出去。

广告

规划助手可以通过观看广告解锁。广告由 Google AdSense 提供。Google 及其合作伙伴可能会使用 Cookie 或类似技术，根据你之前访问本网站或其他网站的情况向你展示广告。你可以在 Google 广告设置 中关闭个性化广告，也可以了解 Google 如何使用这些数据。是否观看广告完全由你决定，游戏不会自动播放广告。

网站托管

网站由 Cloudflare Pages 托管。和所有网站一样，托管服务可能会为了安全和运维记录访问日志（例如 IP 地址和访问时间），这些日志由 Cloudflare 按其隐私政策处理。

儿童

本游戏不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的任何信息。

联系

对本政策有任何问题，请通过 GitHub 联系我们。

Privacy Policy

Metro City · Updated November 2026

What we do not collect

The game needs no account. It does not ask for your name, email or any personal information, and your saved game is never uploaded to our servers.

What is kept in your browser

  • Your saved game and settings (language, sound, music) are kept in your browser's local storage, on your device only. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • How long the planning assistant stays unlocked is kept there too.
  • Which side of the road traffic keeps to is worked out on your device from its time zone and language; nothing is sent anywhere.

Advertising

The planning assistant can be unlocked by watching an advertisement served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ad Settings and read how Google uses this data. Watching an ad is always your choice; the game never plays one by itself.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any website, the host may keep access logs (such as IP addresses and times) for security and operation; Cloudflare handles these under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please reach us on GitHub.
